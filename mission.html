Mission

For the people
in the room.

We make software for communities, players and friend groups. If a thing is good for the room and bad for the chart, we build it anyway.

What we hold to

What we hold to.

Four of them. They are what we settle arguments with, and the reason some obvious features never got built.

01 - Social

The internet is at its best as a group chat.

We design for the people inside the room. A feature is good if the conversation got better, not if the session got longer.

02 - Creative

Playful, colourful, a little odd on purpose.

Software should feel like somebody cared while making it. Hence the characters, the hand-placed shapes, and nothing off a template.

03 - Practical

No dark patterns. No engagement traps.

Leaving is as easy as arriving, and the off switch is where you would look for it.

04 - European

No quarterly graph to please.

Independent, and based in France. Nobody is waiting on a number from us this quarter, so we can take the time to get it right.

The whole idea

Make It Matter.

A test, not a slogan. If what we are about to ship would not matter to the person on the other end of it, it does not go out.

The cost of it

What that rules out.

Things we could do, that would work, and that we are not doing.

An engagement feed

A ranked, endless feed grows the numbers and wrecks the room. Our products show you what the people you chose said, in the order they said it.

Selling what you say to each other

Messages between people are not inventory. Lifecheck exists partly because the usual way of proving someone is human means watching them across the rest of the web.

Passing AI off as a person

Supernova labels what it writes, where you read it rather than in a footnote.

Announcing things that do not exist

Hereld is in build, and every page that mentions it says so. No waiting list, no countdown. It gets a launch when it is ready.

Fortized was the first thing we shipped.

A community platform for gamers and the friend groups around them. Whatever ships next is held to the same four.
