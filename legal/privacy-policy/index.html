Privacy Policy.

What data we collect, why we collect it, and how we keep it safe — across every Swiftaw product, under GDPR.

Last updated: August 2026
On this page
1 Who controls your data 2 What we collect 3 Why we collect it 4 Our goodwill promise 5 Legal basis 6 Sharing & processors 7 Retention 8 Security 9 Your rights 10 Contact

The short version

We collect the data a product needs to work — account details, the content you share, and how you interact with things like Lifecheck. We use it to run and improve our products and to build new ones. We protect it, we keep it only as long as needed, and — as a goodwill commitment — we never sell it to third parties or use it for commercial resale. You have full GDPR rights over it.

1

Who controls your data

Swiftaw, a company based in the Île-de-France region of France, is the data controller for the personal data processed through its products, including Fortized and Lifecheck. Swiftaw operates under French law and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). This Policy is general and applies to all Swiftaw products; a product may publish additional privacy detail specific to it.

2

What we collect

Depending on which product you use, we may collect:

  • Account data — things you give us to have an account, such as your username, email, avatar, and a securely hashed password. This applies to Swiftaw accounts and to product accounts like Fortized accounts.
  • Shared content — the messages, media, and creations you post inside a product such as Fortized, so the product can store and display them for you and the people you share with.
  • Interaction data — how you interact with a product, including the interactions between users and the Swiftaw Lifecheck widget, which we use to tell humans from automated bots.
  • Technical data — limited information your device sends automatically, such as approximate region, browser type and basic logs, used for security and to keep the service running.
What you provide to us
Most of what we hold is what you actively give us — your account details and the content you choose to share. You stay in control of it.
3

Why we collect it

Swiftaw reserves the right to collect data where it is genuinely needed to:

  • Provide and operate the product (for example, storing your avatar and messages so they show up);
  • Keep accounts and content secure, and prevent abuse and fraud (for example, Lifecheck telling humans from bots);
  • Upgrade and improve our systems and features;
  • Research, create and engineer new Swiftaw products.

We aim to collect the minimum a product needs for these purposes.

4

Our goodwill promise

We think being trusted matters more than squeezing value out of your data. So we commit, as a matter of goodwill, that:

Data we collect is not for commercial use, and is not sold to third parties. We use it to run and improve our products and to build new ones — not as a product to be sold on.

This does not remove your legal rights below; it is a promise on top of them.

5

Legal basis for processing

Under GDPR, we process your data on these bases: performance of a contract (to give you the product you signed up for), legitimate interests (to keep our products secure and to improve them, balanced against your rights), consent (where we ask for it, which you can withdraw), and legal obligation (where the law requires us to keep or disclose data).

6

Sharing & processors

We do not sell your data. We share it only with trusted service providers (“processors”) who help us run our products — such as hosting and database infrastructure — and only under contracts that bind them to protect it and use it solely on our instructions. We may also disclose data where the law requires it, or to protect the safety of our users and our products.

7

How long we keep it

We keep personal data only for as long as needed for the purpose it was collected, or as long as the law requires. When you delete content or your account, we remove the associated personal data except for copies we must retain for legal or security reasons. Technical logs are kept for a limited period.

8

Keeping it safe

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data — including hashed passwords, access controls and encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to reduce risk and to respond quickly if something goes wrong, including notifying you and the authorities where the law requires.

Your information, kept safe
Passwords are hashed, connections are encrypted, and access is limited to what is necessary to run the product.
9

Your rights

Under GDPR you have the right to: access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its processing, and receive a copy in a portable format. You can exercise most of these directly in a product's settings, or by contacting us. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority — in France, the CNIL.

10

Contact us

For any privacy question or to make a data request, reach us through your Swiftaw account or the contact details on our product sites, marked “Privacy Request”. We respond in French or English.
