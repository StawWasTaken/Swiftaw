Products Policy.

What Swiftaw makes, how each product's rules fit under our general Terms, and the baseline expectations for all of them.

The short version

Swiftaw's job is to make and deliver digital products — right now, Fortized and Lifecheck. Each product has its own terms and policies for the details, and they all sit under Swiftaw's general Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. This page is the map of how it all fits together.

1

What Swiftaw makes

Swiftaw is a French company whose service is to create and deliver digital products. We design, build and operate them ourselves. Using any Swiftaw product means you agree to Swiftaw's general Terms and Privacy Policy, and to that product's own terms and policies where it has them.

We create the products
Everything under the Swiftaw name is built and run by us — so one set of general rules can sensibly cover all of it.
2

Fortized

Fortized is our gaming-flavoured social chat app — a place to talk, build communities and play. It has accounts, shared content (messages, media, creations), a shop and its own community rules.

Fortized publishes its own Terms of Service and policies on fortized.com. Those product terms reference and sit under these Swiftaw documents, and cover Fortized-specific things (community conduct, the shop, creator tools, and so on).

3

Lifecheck

Lifecheck is our friendly human-verification widget — a captcha that tells humans from bots without being miserable to use. Site owners embed it; visitors complete a quick, playful check.

Lifecheck has its own documentation and integration terms for the people who embed it. Interactions with the widget are handled as described in our Privacy Policy.

4

How the terms stack

Think of it as two layers designed to co-exist:

  • Swiftaw's general documents — the Terms of Service, Privacy Policy and this Products Policy. These stay general and apply to everything we make.
  • Each product's own terms — the product-specific detail (like Fortized's) that references Swiftaw's and adds what is unique to that product.

They are written to fit together, not to contradict. If ever a product term and a general Swiftaw term appear to conflict on the same point, the product-specific term governs for that product, and the general Swiftaw terms govern everything else.

One agreement, two layers. Swiftaw's general terms are the umbrella; a product's own terms are the detail. You accept both when you use a product.
5

Baseline rules for all products

No matter which product you use, you agree to:

  • Use it lawfully and respect other people;
  • Keep your account secure and not share it to evade bans or limits;
  • Not attack, overload or try to bypass a product's security — including abusing or circumventing Lifecheck;
  • Not misuse a product to harm others, spread illegal content, or infringe anyone's rights.

Breaking these, or a product's own policies, can lead to suspension or termination of access.

6

Content & creations

You keep ownership of what you make. By sharing content in a product, you give Swiftaw the licence it needs to host and display that content within the product so it works. Products may also let you create things (like emojis, templates or bots in Fortized) — you are responsible for what you create, and product-specific policies set the rules for it.

7

Purchases & virtual items

Some products offer paid features or virtual items (for example, Fortized's shop and Radiance). Those are governed by that product's own shop and purchase policies, alongside your statutory consumer rights under French and EU law. Virtual items are a licence to use them inside the product, not real-world property.

8

New products

We build new things. When Swiftaw launches a new product, it is automatically covered by our general Terms and Privacy Policy, and we will add it here. Some of the data we collect today (see the Privacy Policy) helps us research and engineer those future products — always within the goodwill promise that we do not sell your data or use it for commercial resale.
