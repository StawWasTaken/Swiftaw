Using the marks

Nothing unusual. It comes down to leaving the logo as it is.

Please do

  • Write it Swiftaw. One word, one capital.
  • Leave clear space around the mark, at least the height of the S.
  • Use the white or black wordmark when colour will not read.
  • Say Hereld is not open yet when you name it.

Please do not

  • Write SwiftAW, Swift Aw, SWIFTAW or Swiftaw Inc.
  • Recolour, stretch, rotate, outline or add effects to the mark.
  • Put the colour wordmark on a busy photograph or a mid grey.
  • Use our marks in a way that suggests we endorse something.
