what it is

One AI, doing two jobs.

Supernova is a generative AI of our own. You'll be able to talk to it like any assistant, and behind the scenes the very same brain runs the automated systems across everything we make.

For people

An assistant you can talk to

Ask it things, get it to write, plan, explain or lend a hand. A generative AI in the same family as the ones you already know, but built and shaped by us, in our own voice.

For our products

The brain behind the scenes

The same intelligence powers our automation: telling people from bots in Lifecheck, keeping things friendly in Fortized, and anything new we dream up. One model, wired in everywhere.

how we're teaching it

Fed from our world, and the open one.

Supernova learns from two places: the things we build, and the vast amount of freely available knowledge out on the web. The more it sees, the more useful it becomes.

Our own world

The projects we make teach it what we're about and how we like to help, handled with care for people's privacy.

The open web

Freely available public knowledge broadens what it understands, so it can hold its own on almost anything.

Grows with us

Every new thing we ship becomes something Supernova can learn from. It gets sharper as Swiftaw does.

We take the data seriously. Where Supernova learns from our products, we handle it with care for privacy, and we'll be clear about what it does and doesn't touch. It's early, and we'd rather earn trust than rush it.
