how it learns

It learns normal, then notices the odd one out.

Supernova doesn't work from a hand-written list of "this is a bot". It watches how real sessions behave, builds a picture of normal, and treats whatever sits far outside it with suspicion.

01

Reads real behaviour

Every Lifecheck pass leaves a tiny, anonymous trace of how a session moved and felt. Supernova learns from those, never from who you are.

02

Finds the outliers

It groups the ordinary together and flags what lands nowhere near the crowd. No labelled examples required, so it works from day one.

03

Gets sharper over time

The more real sessions it sees, the tighter its sense of normal, and the fewer actual people it ever needs to bother.

We're honest about the stage: Supernova is in training. For now it watches and scores in the background while Lifecheck's own checks still make the call. It earns more say only once it's proven.
what makes it ours

Small, kind, and quietly effective.

Privacy first

Signals are anonymous and short-lived. No cookies, no profiles built on you, nothing sold on.

Advisory to start

Supernova won't block a soul on its own yet. It advises while it learns, so real people are never caught out.

One brain, many uses

It starts with Lifecheck, but it's built to help wherever telling humans from bots matters across Swiftaw.

Ours end to end

Built and run by the team behind Fortized and Lifecheck, on our own infrastructure. No black box rented from elsewhere.

where it's headed

We're building this in the open.

Supernova is early, and we'd rather show the plan than pretend it's finished. Here's the honest order of things.

Now

Learning the shape of normal

Gathering anonymous session signals from Lifecheck and building the first model of what a real person looks like.

Next

Quiet background scoring

Supernova scores sessions as they happen, still advisory, so we can check it against reality before it changes anything.

Later

A sharper Lifecheck

Its read starts feeding Lifecheck directly, so real people breeze through even faster and bots meet more friction.

Someday

Across Swiftaw

The same brain, lending a hand wherever we need to tell a person from a script, kept just as private.
