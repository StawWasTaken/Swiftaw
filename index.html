Three rules

How we build.

01

For the people in the room.

The internet is at its best when it feels like a group chat. We design for the people in it, not the chart drawn around them.

02

Healthy by default.

No dark patterns and no endless hooks. If a button is designed so you press it by accident, it is a bad button.

03

Slow where it counts.

No quarterly graph to please. We would rather ship one thing that holds up than five that need apologising for.
